# Tidings Voice — Quick Reference

For deeper context, tone-flex table, approved domain terms, and the PR review checklist, read [`/workspace/docs/brand/voice.md`](../../../../docs/brand/voice.md).

## Voice constants (always)

- **Observant, not evaluative.** State numbers. Do not celebrate, scold, congratulate, warn.
- **Second person, present tense.** "You" — never "I" or "we" in product UI.
- **Sentence case** on every label, button, heading, pill, nav item.
- **No exclamation marks.** Ever.
- **No emoji** in product UI.
- **Tagline:** *Your spending, delivered.*

## Banned words (do not ship)

`unlock`, `supercharge`, `crush`, `conquer`, `boost`, `skyrocket`, `level up`,
`streak`, `win`, `level`, `score`, `achievement`, `challenge`,
`urgent`, `critical`, `alert!`, `warning!`, `danger`,
`oops`, `whoops`, `awesome`, `amazing`, `magical`,
`seamless`, `effortless`, `revolutionary`, `disrupt`.

## Product name

**Tidings** — always capital T, never abbreviated, never lowercase. Sentence case applies to the rest of the sentence, not to the name. The public repository is `tidings`; user-facing copy is always Tidings.

## Three before / after examples

**Before:** "🎉 You crushed your dining budget this month! 23% under!"
**After:** "Dining is $48 under target this month."

**Before:** "WARNING! Your groceries spending is 105% of budget — take action!"
**After:** "Groceries is $14 over ceiling."

**Before:** "Welcome aboard! Let's unlock your financial superpowers 🚀"
**After:** "Forward your bank's transaction emails to the address shown in Settings. Transactions appear here as they arrive."

The pattern: number first, fact plain, no adjective, no exclamation, no emoji.

## The five-bullet PR check

1. No exclamation marks in any new or changed string.
2. Sentence case on every label / button / heading / pill.
3. No banned words from the list above.
4. Observant, not evaluative — no celebrating, scolding, congratulating, warning.
5. Voice consistency — the new string sounds like the same writer wrote three nearby existing strings.
